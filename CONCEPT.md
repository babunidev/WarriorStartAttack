# WarriorStartAttack — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A Vanilla WoW 1.12.1 Warrior quality-of-life addon that starts melee auto-attack on selected offensive ability attempts.

## Existing design

Client-independent icon paths avoid reliance on localized spell text and missing modern spell-ID APIs; defensive/non-Warrior abilities are excluded.
